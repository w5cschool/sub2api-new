## Design Context

### Users
Developers and operations teams use this self-hosted API gateway to administer their own integrations. They need a calm, unambiguous explanation of what the site is, where to find documentation, and how to reach the operator without being asked for unrelated credentials or payment information.

### Brand Personality
Clear, accountable, and technical. The interface should build confidence through specific boundaries and plain language rather than urgency, scarcity, or third-party brand association.

### Aesthetic Direction
Quiet, editorial utility: a warm light canvas, restrained green accent, strong hierarchy, and simple line icons. The landing page is an identity and trust page, not a conversion funnel. It supports dark mode and avoids borrowed product identity, pricing pressure, or promotional visual language.

### Design Principles
1. State the operator's own service and responsibility before discussing integrations.
2. Never imply affiliation, endorsement, or authorization from third-party providers.
3. Never request passwords, one-time codes, payment details, private keys, or third-party API credentials outside their explicit product flows.
4. Prefer documentation and accountable support paths over urgency-driven calls to action.
5. Keep the homepage controlled by reviewed application code; do not render arbitrary administrator-supplied HTML or external pages there.
